# Product Vision: Kintai

🌐 **English** · [Français](vision.fr.md) · [日本語](vision.ja.md)

## 🎯 The Mission
Kintai's mission is to liberate small to medium-sized retail and hospitality businesses from the friction of archaic shift management. We provide a bridge between the "printed Excel sheet" and high-end enterprise resource planning (ERP) systems, offering a solution that is as simple as it is powerful.

## 🌟 The "Sovereign SaaS" Philosophy
Unlike traditional SaaS platforms that lock data in a black box, Kintai believes in **Data Sovereignty**. Our customers are not just "rows in a table"; they are owners of their digital infrastructure.
- **Physical Isolation:** Every customer (owner) has their own isolated database.
- **Freedom of Exit:** Customers can move from our hosted SaaS to their own self-hosted infrastructure in minutes, with zero data loss.
- **Privacy by Design:** Security is not an afterthought; isolation is baked into the deployment model.

## 📍 Target Markets
1. **Primary: Japan.** Addressing the specific cultural and legal nuances of the Japanese retail sector (CJK support, local payroll rules).
2. **Secondary: Global SMEs.** Businesses that value privacy, simplicity, and low overhead.

## 🚀 Strategic Pillars
- **Lightweight Core:** A fast, PHP-native core that can run on anything from a cheap VPS to a managed container.
- **Modular by design, open by default:** Advanced features (Messaging, Daily Reports, and future additions like AI-assisted scheduling) live as feature-flagged bundles rather than bloating the core — but every bundle ships AGPL-3.0 in this repository, free to self-host.
- **Trust-based Growth:** Attract customers through open-source transparency, retain them through the convenience and reliability of our managed SaaS orchestration — never through code they can't otherwise access.

## 🌈 The Future
Kintai aims to become the "WordPress of Workforce Management"—a standard platform that anyone can install, but everyone prefers to use on our optimized, managed cloud.
